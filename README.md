# 💬 Organization Discussions Hub

Welcome to 128x64's central hub for discussions, brainstorming, and community Q&A! This repository is explicitly set up to act as a community forum using **GitHub Discussions**. 

We use this space instead of issues to connect with contributors, answer questions, and collaborate on future ideas.

---

## 🗺️ How to Get Started

To start a new conversation or find answers, navigate to the **[Discussions Tab](../../discussions)** at the top of this repository.

### 🗂️ Discussion Categories

We organize our conversations into specific categories to keep things neat:

*   **👋 Welcome & Introductions:** Introduce yourself to the community and meet fellow contributors.
*   **💡 Ideas & Feature Requests:** Share your feature concepts, enhancements, or wild ideas.
*   **❓ Q&A / Support:** Stuck on something? Ask questions and get help from the community.
*   **📢 Announcements:** Stay up to date with official project updates, releases, and news.
*   **🎨 Show and Tell:** Show off what you built or achieved using this project!

---

## 📜 Community Guidelines

To maintain a healthy, welcoming, and productive environment, please ensure your interactions align with our standards:

1.  **Be respectful:** Treat everyone with kindness and constructive empathy.
2.  **Search before posting:** Your question or idea might have already been discussed! Check existing threads first.
3.  **Upvote helpful answers:** If someone resolves your question, mark it as the **Accepted Answer** so others can find it easily.
4.  **Keep it relevant:** Use the appropriate category for your post to keep the forum organized.

---

## 🤝 Contributing

If you want to contribute code, documentation, or bug reports rather than joining a casual chat:
*   Check out our **[Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/128x64/.github/blob/main/community/CONTRIBUTING.md)**.
*   Open an official bug tracking ticket in our **[Issues Tab](../../issues)**.

Thank you for being part of our community! Happy discussing! 🚀
